M5.6 introduces a 15-page representative vertical slice with three nested branches,
examples, one guided exercise, stable Course navigation, exact formal provenance and
honest external-classification states. It is not a complete course or a deployment
authorization.

Every published page has one English canonical route. Portuguese remains explicitly
unavailable, so there are no fabricated `/pt/` pages or `hreflang` claims. OntoMathPRO
and arXiv are unavailable, while MSC 2020 remains license-needs-review; Course is the
fail-safe navigation projection.

The site stays static and bounded. It does not run Lean remotely, mutate taxonomies,
own curriculum identities, or replace the exact content and formal authorities it cites.
