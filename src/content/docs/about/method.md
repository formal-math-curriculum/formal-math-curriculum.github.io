---
title: Authority and claims
description: What the site may and may not claim.
---

Curriculum identity comes from the adopted Project 1 records. Editorial and pedagogical
content comes from the governed `content` repository. Lean declarations and proof
evidence come from exact revisions of the `lean` repository.

Generated navigation, routes, rendered mathematics, and search indexes are publication
outputs. They never replace those authorities.

The public outline modes are Course, OntoMathPRO, MSC 2020, arXiv, and Lean / mathlib.
An unavailable mapping is shown as unavailable; it is never invented.
