---
title: Authority and claims
description: What the site may and may not claim.
---

Curriculum identity comes from the adopted Project 1 records. Editorial and pedagogical
content comes from the governed `content` repository. Lean declarations and proof
evidence come from exact revisions of the `lean` repository.

Generated navigation, routes, rendered mathematics, and search indexes are publication
outputs. They never replace those authorities.

The public outline modes are Course, OntoMathPRO, MSC 2020, arXiv, and Lean / mathlib.
An unavailable mapping is shown as unavailable; it is never invented.

The current representative slice consumes `P5-M5.6-CONTENT-v1` at the exact merged
content commit recorded in the public build provenance. It publishes 15 English
canonical pages and bounded formal-navigation records. Portuguese is recorded as
unavailable and receives no route. The same content identity may appear more than once
in Course order, but every placement resolves to one canonical page.

Rendered, LaTeX and Lean views share a governed block identity. “Exact” and “scoped”
describe the reviewed correspondence of that block; neither label expands to a whole
topic, whole curriculum candidate or remote proof service. Learner readiness, Course
order and formal imports remain distinct authorities.
